Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Squad.com collects, uses, and protects your personal data. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at dataprotection@squad.com.

1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

For individual users who sign up directly, Squad.com is the data controller — we decide how your information is collected and used to provide our service.

For business users, the contracting company is the controller, and Squad.com acts as the data operator, processing data according to that company's instructions.

We also work with trusted third-party operators (such as hosting providers, chatbots, and AI models) that help us run the platform. Our contracts require them to handle your data securely.

2. How We Keep Your Data Secure

Your data is stored on AWS and Azure servers with restricted access. These servers are located in the United States, which may involve an international transfer of your personal data.

We use encryption, access controls, and two-step authentication to protect our databases. No content you enter is used to train AI models — it is used only to fulfill your request and then stored separately.

3. What Data We Collect

To register and use Squad.com, we collect:

  • Individual users: full name and email address.
  • Business users: representative's full name and email, company name, tax ID, and address.
  • Optional: photos and voice recordings, if you choose to create digital avatars.
  • Automatically collected: access logs (IP address, date and time of access) and cookie data.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and identify your account
  • Generate digital characters (avatars)
  • Provide our services and integrations
  • Send sales, marketing, and product updates
  • Draft contracts and meet tax obligations
  • Provide customer support

5. Third-Party AI Services

Squad.com uses third-party AI services to power its AI agents across conversational tools, marketing tools, and other product categories. When you use these AI-powered features, the following data may be sent to third-party AI service providers:

  • Text inputs, prompts, and messages you submit through AI-powered features
  • Any content you provide within AI-powered tools for processing

This data is sent to third-party AI service providers solely to process your request and deliver the feature. Your data is not used by these providers to train their AI models.

By creating an account and using Squad.com, you consent to this data sharing. You may withdraw this consent at any time by discontinuing use of the AI-powered features or by contacting us at dataprotection@squad.com.

We require all third-party AI service providers to handle your data securely and with the same or equal level of data protection that we provide.

6. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except with service providers necessary to operate the platform, when required by law or court order, or when you have given consent.

7. Access Logs

We keep access logs (date, time, and IP address of platform use) for a minimum of 6 months, as required by Brazilian law (Federal Law nº 12.965/2014).

8. Your Rights

At any time, you can ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we are processing your data
  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated data
  • Anonymize, block, or delete unnecessary data
  • Transfer your data to another provider
  • Delete data processed with your consent
  • Learn who we have shared your data with
  • Withdraw your consent

9. Cancelling Your Account

Individual users: email support@squad.com to cancel your account.

Business users: requests must be made directly to your company, which is the data controller.

Once your account is cancelled, we will delete your data promptly, except for information we are legally required to retain (such as access logs).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version applies from the date it is published. If a change requires your consent, we will ask for it directly.

11. Contact

For privacy questions: dataprotection@squad.com
For general support: support@squad.com
